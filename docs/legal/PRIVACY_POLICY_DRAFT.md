## Children

The service is for adults **[confirm minimum age]**. A parent can add a
child's measurements to get size recommendations. We don't accept children's
photos on those profiles, and photo try-on is switched off for children's
profiles and children's products. Children's measurements are still personal
data about a child. **[Lawyer: review against COPPA, GDPR-K and local rules,
including whether parental consent must be verified. A shopper could still
upload a child's photo as their own; the software can't detect that.]**

## Your choices

- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete account. We sign you out and
  erase your account, photos and try-ons after 30 days. Log in within those
  30 days to cancel. Brand accounts lose their store and products too.
- **Delete individual items:** family profiles and wardrobe items can be
  deleted at any time.
